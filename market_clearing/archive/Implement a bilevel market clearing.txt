Implement a bilevel market clearing model with the following structure:

LAYER 1 - CLEARING PHASE (Ex-Ante Market):
- Markets clear using wind FORECASTS with noise (no perfect foresight override)
- Produces: q (adjustments), g_planned (positions), λ (prices), Qch/Qdis/SOC schedules
- All generators bid based on forecasted wind
- Gate closure locks Base/Mid/Solar; Peak and Wind remain flexible
- This is the financial/market layer

LAYER 2 - DELIVERY PHASE (Ex-Post Physical Reality):
- Wind REALIZES differently from forecast (use Q_gen_full with no noise)
- Peak plant re-optimizes jointly with Battery to balance forecast errors
- Solve second optimization with:
  * Fixed: Wind = realized, Base/Mid/Solar = committed (g_planned)
  * Optimize: Peak + Battery (Qch, Qdis, SOC)
  * Objective: Minimize peak generation cost + battery cycling costs
  * Constraints: Energy balance, SOC dynamics, power/energy limits
- Produces: g_actual (physical dispatch), SOC_actual (physical battery state)

STATE CONTINUITY:
- Financial: g_planned (future hours) → next Q_prev
- Physical battery: SOC_actual[reclear_freq] → next SOC_init
- Physical generation: g_actual[reclear_freq] → g_last_executed (for ramping)

COST TRACKING:
- Market settlement: g_planned × clearing_prices (financial layer)
- Physical production: g_actual × marginal_costs (physical layer)  
- Imbalance: Difference shows cost of forecast errors
